Night Symptom Reference

Find out what your morning symptoms mean and how to fix them.

Morning Headaches

Cause: Often caused by a pillow that is too high, pushing the head forward and restricting blood flow.

Fix: Lower pillow height or use a contour dip.

Numb Arms & Hands

Cause: Sleeping on a mattress that is too firm or a pillow lacking shoulder room, compressing nerves.

Fix: Softer mattress topper or memory foam pillow.

Tossing & Turning

Cause: Pressure point buildup from non-conforming materials (like cheap polyester fill).

Fix: Solid memory foam that responds to body heat.

Pillow Height Cheat Sheet

Quick reference guide for standard pillow lofts.

Sleep Position Recommended Loft (Height) Why?
Side Sleepers High (4 to 6 inches) Needs to fill the large gap between the shoulder and ear to keep the spine straight.
Back Sleepers Medium (3 to 4 inches) Needs to support the natural curve of the neck without pushing the head too far forward.
Stomach Sleepers Low (1 to 2 inches) Requires a very flat pillow (or no pillow) to prevent the neck from twisting backward.

Pillow Specification Glossary

Understand the technical terms used in our pillow reviews.

Loft

The height or thickness of a pillow. Getting the right loft is the most important factor for spinal alignment.

Memory Foam

A viscoelastic material that reacts to body heat and pressure, allowing it to mold to the user's unique shape.

Cervical Contour

A curved design (like a butterfly shape) that specifically targets the natural curve of the cervical spine (neck).

ILD (Indentation Load Deflection)

A measurement of foam firmness. A lower ILD means softer foam, while a higher ILD means firmer foam.
