Why Pillow Shape Matters for Sleeping Comfort

Your pillow's job isn't just to feel soft; it's to bridge the gap between your mattress and your head, keeping your spine in a neutral line. If your pillow is too high or too flat, your neck bends at an unnatural angle for hours. Over time, this poor sleeping posture can lead to tension, stiffness, and discomfort. Ergonomic pillows aim to solve this by offering structured support that adapts to your sleeping position.

Key Features & Benefits

Butterfly Contour

The standout feature is its contoured shape. The pillow features a central depression that cradles the head, while the raised edges support the natural curve of the neck. This design helps keep the airways open.

Premium Memory Foam

Derila uses a solid memory foam block rather than shredded fill. This ensures the pillow holds its shape throughout the night, providing consistent support without going flat.

Cooling Cover

Memory foam is known for retaining heat. To counter this, the Derila Ergo includes a breathable, removable cover designed to promote airflow and help regulate temperature.

Sleeping Positions & Cervical Alignment

For back sleepers, the central dip allows the head to rest level while the neck is supported, preventing the head from being pushed forward. For side sleepers, the higher edges of the pillow fill the space between the shoulder and the ear, keeping the spine horizontally straight.
