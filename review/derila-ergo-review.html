What is Derila Ergo?

Derila Ergo is a contoured memory-foam pillow designed to support the head and neck during sleep. Its shape differs from a traditional rectangular pillow, with sculpted areas intended to help users maintain a comfortable sleeping position.

Important: This is a consumer product review. A pillow is not a treatment for sleep apnea or other medical conditions. If you have persistent pain, breathing problems during sleep, or suspected sleep apnea, speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
